# Torrey Pines Institute (TPIMS)

Port St. Lucie, FL · [tpims.org](https://tpims.org)

Torrey Pines Institute for Molecular Studies (TPIMS) is a non-profit research center dedicated to basic research that advances the understanding of human disease and improves human health. At the core of its work are large compound libraries, including peptide research libraries, and screening technologies that help scientists identify promising new drug candidates faster. TPIMS applies its compound library and drug discovery platform to pain, cancer, Alzheimer's disease and the opioid epidemic. It was selected as a Specialized Center in the National Cancer Institute's Experimental Therapeutics (NExT) Program Chemical Biology Consortium and has research partnerships with Zealand Pharma and the Tri-Institutional Therapeutics Discovery Institute. CEO Richard A. Houghten, Ph.D., was inducted into the Florida Inventors Hall of Fame. Learn more at https://tpims.org.
